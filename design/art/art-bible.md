## 1. Visual Identity Statement

Every moving form should read as a few living brush gestures on open paper: dark characters, pale fleeting force, generous silence. The user's original elder is the sole style anchor.

- Combat readability: a thumbnail must distinguish elder, ninja, dart and crescent by silhouette before texture.
- Preserved feel: visual trajectories follow existing simulation; attractive brushwork never changes movement or collision.
- Ink coherence: natural pigment accumulation, lost edges and limited feibai belong to the silhouette itself, not a texture over geometric blocks.

## 2. Mood and Atmosphere

Title: quiet warm-white field, deep black title, one cinnabar mark; contemplative anticipation. Combat: same stable warm light, high character contrast, short restrained pale effects; quick and lucid. Ultimate: existing timing and staging preserved, a momentary cinnabar accent and forceful gesture; no modern neon. Defeat: settle the composition, show score/title with clear white space; dignified rather than noisy.

## 3. Shape Language

Hero keeps frozen long brows, beard and billowing robe. Ninjas use a compact hood and irregular continuous ink mass; joints dissolve into connected sleeve/pant strokes. Airborne knees fold close to the waist. Avoid rectangles, regular circles, polygonal elbows, neat V-neck and shoe blocks. Eyes are small outer-high/inner-low triangular reserves. Environments are incomplete organic strokes. UI combines vertical inscription rhythm with simple readable numbers and small seals.

## 4. Color System

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Warm white | #f6f2e8 | Empty field, reserved white |
| Deep ink | #242321 | Characters, critical text, darts |
| Dry ink grey | #68675f | Character brush breaks |
| Diluted ink | #9c9b92 | Translucent blade and mountains |
| Cinnabar | #a54e3b | Nose, life marks, red enemy |
| Mineral green | #46644b | Green enemy clothing |
| Pale silver | #d9d8ce | Rare blade accent |

Color accompanies motion: red throws two darts, green descends quickly; do not depend on hue alone. No blue glow. UI contrast outranks faux-calligraphy flourish. Coloring must preserve white eye reserves and dark dead X marks.

## 5. Character Direction

Frozen protagonist actions are directly reused. New ninja has a large hood and tiny feature field; sleeve and body silhouettes are broken by believable brush bristles. One black master and deterministic recoloring/rig maintain identity across all three colors. Four cycles (air, patrol, air throw, ground throw) use three frames each. Death arms rise in V, black X eyes remain legible, actual waist cut inside torso produces independently moving halves. Patrol feet stay short; contact support is derived from alpha rather than old rectangular proxies.

## 6. Environment

At least approximately 80% open warm white. Two or three faint mountain gestures on one side, no detailed scenery. Ground is one horizontal variable-density stroke placed at the real collision height; minor dry brush below it supplies thickness. No temples, pine forests, railings, patterned tiles or busy paper stains.

## 7. UI / HUD

Bottom information band belongs to the scroll: clean paper, ink separators, score largest. Life uses three small cinnabar marks; charge four stages remain mechanically identical. New title briefly appears and returns at results. Permanent left/right buttons remain invisible. Ready text is 双按·绝杀. Touch feedback is short and unobtrusive. Preserve existing opening, enemy entry and first ultimate tutorial. Body text should stay readable at 390 CSS px width (typically ≥13 px).

## 8. Asset Standards

PNG RGBA straight alpha, transparent outside = 0, RGB zeroed for fully transparent pixels; natural fractional edge alpha retained. Ninja: 576×576, origin [288,376], 2 texture px per old Canvas unit. Files retain existing integration names under color/action folders; this explicit compatibility exception supersedes the generic template name format. Sheets use three columns for cycles, two for death. No baked paper or checkerboard. One source rig; mirrored at rendering time. Suggested playback air6/patrol8/throw12 FPS; actual AI timing unchanged. Runtime can downsample ninjas to 288 px for mobile; exported originals remain full size. Expanded PNG memory estimate must be measured, not assumed. Assets may not introduce gameplay dependencies. Default camera supports portrait 390×844 and wider desktop.

## 9. Reference Direction and Prohibitions

Only project sources: (1) original_source.png supplies natural ink edges and density; (2) frozen v05 flight supplies silhouette readability/white brow reserves; (3) moon_v01 supplies concentration of pigment versus pale force. These are complementary views of the same approved visual identity. No external style graft. Avoid perfectly outlined costumes, geometric ragdoll-looking anatomy, airbrushed gradients, bright energy loops, decorative particle clouds. Do not trace old Canvas rectangles and call them final brush art.
